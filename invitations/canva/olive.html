Every love story is beautiful

But ours is my favourite

Sofia and Matteo met in the most unexpected way — reaching for the very last umbrella in a tiny Florentine bookshop during a summer storm. What started as a polite argument turned into an espresso, then a dinner, then a love story. Over the years they travelled, laughed, built a home and planted a garden full of olive trees. Now they can't wait to celebrate this beautiful chapter with the people who mean the most to them.
