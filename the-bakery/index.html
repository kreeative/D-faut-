    Our name

    Spelled in bread and buttercream

    The B is a baguette

    Six scores down its length, like the ones we cut into every loaf before it goes in the oven.

    The e is a cupcake

    A swirl of buttercream on a pleated paper case. Look again: it’s also our mascot’s head.

    The rest is drawn by hand

    Ears of wheat, pretzels, croissants and slices of toast, drawn with one pen. They’re printed on every bag and on the stickers that close it.

    Meet the baker

    Our mascot has a cupcake for a head and is always out of breath from running bags to the counter. You’ll find them waving from every sleeve.

    The bags

    A modest promise, printed big

    Cookies go home in a bag that says “Might be delicious!”. Croissants get “Might be scrumptious!”. We keep the promise modest and let the bake do the talking.
